# Cultural Value Orientations | 三位学者文化维度理论

## Kluckhohn & Strodtbeck | K&S · 5 dimensions / 15 types

### Human Nature Orientation | 人性取向

_What is the inborn character of human nature?｜人性本质如何？_

#### Evil but perfectible | 本恶但可完善

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Original sin; people are born sinful (原罪观：认为人出生时带有罪)
        
    - Salvation, discipline and self-control (通过救赎、自律和自我约束改善)
        
    - Confession and moral improvement (承认过错，并借由行善完善自身)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Christianity / Christian tradition (基督教文化传统)
        
    - Puritans (清教徒)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Adam and Eve; guilt culture (亚当与夏娃；罪感文化)
        

#### Mixture of good and evil | 善恶兼有

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Everyone has both good and bad sides (人人同时具有善与恶的一面)
        
    - Human behavior needs supervision (认为行为需要约束与监督)
        
    - Checks and balances restrain wrongdoing (以制度制衡防止权力滥用)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Europeans (欧洲人)
        
    - U.S. middle-class subculture (美国中产阶层文化（补充阅读）)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Legislature and press monitor officials (立法机关与媒体监督官员)
        

#### Good but corruptible | 本善但可腐化

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - People are born good and innocent (认为人出生时善良纯真)
        
    - Habits and society may corrupt people (后天习惯与社会环境可能使人变坏)
        
    - Parents and teachers should be moral models (父母、教师等应成为道德榜样)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Chinese Confucian tradition (中国儒家传统)
        
    - Native Americans / Hindu (basically good in comparison chart) (美洲原住民／印度教：对照表中归入“基本善”)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - “Human nature is good at birth” (“人之初，性本善”)
        
    - Mencius’ mother moving three times (孟母三迁)
        
- **Terminology note | 术语注意**
    
    - “Basically good” is a comparison label, not identical wording (“基本善”是比较表术语，不等同于完整的“本善但可腐化”)
        

### Person–Nature Orientation | 人与自然关系取向

_How do humans relate to nature?｜人与自然如何相处？_

#### Subjugation to nature | 屈从自然

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Natural forces and fate exceed human control (自然力量和命运超出人的控制)
        
    - Accept, respect and be humble toward nature (接受自然、敬畏自然、保持谦逊)
        
    - Disasters reveal human powerlessness (灾害体现人类面对自然的局限)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - India; parts of South America (印度；南美部分地区)
        
    - Native Americans (noted in comparison table) (美洲原住民（笔记对照表）)
        
    - Spanish Americans of the U.S. Southwest (美国西南部西班牙裔美国人（补充阅读）)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Sheep herders cannot stop severe storms (牧羊人无法阻止暴风雨)
        

#### Harmony with nature | 与自然和谐

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Humans belong to the natural order (人是自然整体的一部分)
        
    - Follow natural rhythms; maintain balance (顺应自然规律并保持平衡)
        
    - Favor coexistence over domination (强调与自然共存而非征服)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - China; Japan; Thailand (中国；日本；泰国)
        
    - Native American cultures (美洲原住民文化)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Chinese gardens; traditional Chinese medicine (中国园林；中医调和)
        
    - Unity of heaven and humanity (天人和谐／天人感应)
        

#### Mastery over nature | 控制自然

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Humans can control and change the environment (人能够控制、改造环境)
        
    - Science and technology solve problems (依靠科学技术解决问题)
        
    - Increase efficiency, comfort and security (改善效率、舒适度与安全)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - United States / North America (美国／北美)
        
    - Western industrialized cultures (西方工业化文化)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Dams, engineering and medical treatment (修筑水坝、工程改造和疾病治疗)
        

### Time Orientation | 时间取向

_Which time period receives priority?｜更重视哪个时间维度？_

#### Past-oriented | 过去取向

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - History, tradition and ancestors matter (重视历史、传统与祖先)
        
    - Heritage and established customs bring security (文化传承与既有惯例带来安全感)
        
    - Respect family ties; cyclical view of time (尊重家族联系；部分文化重时间循环)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - China; Japan (中国；日本)
        
    - United Kingdom / England; France; Belgium (英国／英格兰；法国；比利时)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Ancestor respect and British traditions (敬祖传统与英国历史传承)
        

#### Present-oriented | 现在取向

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - The here and now matters most (当下时刻最重要)
        
    - Future is uncertain or beyond control (未来难以预测或掌控)
        
    - Enjoy the moment; spontaneity and flexibility (享受当下；强调自发性与灵活性)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Philippines; Spain; Latin American cultures (菲律宾；西班牙；拉丁美洲文化)
        
    - African Americans (in notes) (非裔美国人（笔记）)
        
    - Navajo of northern Arizona (reading) (亚利桑那州北部纳瓦霍人（补充阅读）)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - “Only the here and now is real” (“唯有此时此地是真实的”)
        

#### Future-oriented | 未来取向

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Planning, progress and goal-setting (强调规划、进步与目标)
        
    - Change, optimism and taking chances (乐观、拥抱变化、敢于尝试)
        
    - Linear time; punctuality and efficiency (线性时间观；重视时间与效率)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - United States (dominant culture) (美国（主导文化）)
        
    - Puritan tradition in the U.S. (美国清教徒传统)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - “Time is money” (“时间就是金钱”)
        

### Activity Orientation | 活动取向

_What is the preferred mode of activity?｜人应该怎样行动？_

#### Doing | 做事取向

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Action, accomplishment and efficiency (重视行动、成就与效率)
        
    - Judge results by measurable performance (倾向用可量化的成果评价表现)
        
    - Planning, decisions, schedules and deadlines (强调计划、决策、日程与期限)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - United States (dominant culture) (美国（主导文化）)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - “What have you accomplished?” (“你取得了什么成果？”)
        

#### Being | 存在取向

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Identity and spontaneous lived experience (强调身份及当下自然体验)
        
    - Relationships and enjoyment over measurable output (人际关系与生活体验比绩效更重要)
        
    - Status, role and character may matter (地位、社会角色、品格具有重要性)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Mexico; Latino cultures (墨西哥；拉丁美洲文化)
        
    - Chinese comparison (in notes) (中国文化（笔记中的中西对照）)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Long conversations with family and friends (与亲朋好友长时间交谈)
        

#### Being-in-Becoming | 成为／自我发展取向

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Develop the inner self and the whole person (重视内在自我与人格整体发展)
        
    - Spiritual growth over external achievement (精神成长高于外在名利)
        
    - Meditation and self-cultivation (冥想、修行与自我完善)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Buddhist monks / Buddhism (佛教僧侣／佛教)
        
    - Hindu traditions (印度教传统)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Meditation to develop the self (通过冥想实现自我发展)
        

### Relational Orientation | 人际关系取向

_How should people relate to others?｜如何组织人与人之间的关系？_

#### Individualism | 个人取向

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Individual goals take precedence (个人目标优先)
        
    - People are independent and self-reliant (个体独立、自主、自我负责)
        
    - Personal opinions and decisions matter (重视个人意见与自行决策)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - United States (美国)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Personal achievements and self-expression (个人成就与自我表达)
        

#### Lineality | 世系／纵向群体取向

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Continuity of the group across generations (重视群体跨世代延续)
        
    - Group membership and inherited position (身份与群体归属、世袭地位相关)
        
    - Group goals outweigh individual goals (群体目标优先于个人目标)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - European aristocracy (欧洲贵族传统)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Inherited nobility and long-standing lineages (世袭贵族与家族传承)
        
- **Terminology note | 术语注意**
    
    - The other notes use “Hierarchy” (China), a different emphasis (另一份笔记用“Hierarchy／等级”（中国）一词，侧重点不同)
        

#### Collaterality | 横向群体取向

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Immediate groups are central (重视个人当前所属群体)
        
    - Team loyalty and common goals (强调团队忠诚与共同目标)
        
    - Group interest before individual distinction (群体利益优先，避免个人过度突出)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Japan: company / university groups (日本：企业／大学群体)
        
    - Teamwork cultures (reading) (团队合作文化（补充阅读）)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - A Japanese employee identifies with the company (日本员工认同所属企业)
        
- **Terminology note | 术语注意**
    
    - Other notes use “Group” (Native Americans), not exactly identical (另一份笔记用“Group／群体”（美洲原住民），并非完全同义)

## Geert Hofstede | 4 dimensions / 8 types

### Individualism vs. Collectivism | 个人主义与集体主义

_“I” or “We”?｜个人目标还是群体目标？_

#### Individualism | 个人主义

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - “I” consciousness; personal goals first (“我”意识；个人目标优先)
        
    - Privacy, independence and individual rights (强调隐私、独立与个人权利)
        
    - Initiative, achievement and competition (重视个人主动性、成就与竞争)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - United States; Australia; United Kingdom (美国；澳大利亚；英国)
        
    - Canada; New Zealand; Netherlands (加拿大；新西兰；荷兰)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Individuals may change group memberships (个人较容易变更组织归属)
        

#### Collectivism | 集体主义

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - “We” consciousness; group interests first (“我们”意识；群体利益优先)
        
    - Strong in-groups and lifelong loyalty (紧密内群体与持久忠诚)
        
    - Cooperation and group decision-making (强调合作与集体决策)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Venezuela; Colombia; Pakistan (委内瑞拉；哥伦比亚；巴基斯坦)
        
    - Singapore; Thailand; Taiwan (China) (新加坡；泰国；中国台湾地区)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Extended families protect members in return for loyalty (大家庭提供保护，成员以忠诚回报)
        

### Uncertainty Avoidance | 不确定性规避

_How comfortable are people with ambiguity?｜如何面对不确定性？_

#### High uncertainty avoidance | 高不确定性规避

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Low tolerance of ambiguity and uncertainty (较难容忍模糊与不确定)
        
    - More formal rules, rituals and clear standards (需要更多规则、仪式和明确标准)
        
    - Greater anxiety; “different is dangerous” (焦虑倾向较高；“不同意味着危险”)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Greece; Portugal; Belgium (希腊；葡萄牙；比利时)
        
    - Japan; Peru; France (日本；秘鲁；法国)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Not the same as refusing every risk (高不确定性规避不等于回避一切风险)
        

#### Low uncertainty avoidance | 低不确定性规避

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Accept ambiguity and unfamiliar situations (较能接受模糊与陌生情境)
        
    - Flexible rules and openness to new ideas (规则较灵活，愿接纳新观点)
        
    - Lower stress; “different is curious” (压力较低；“不同值得好奇”)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Singapore; Denmark; Sweden (新加坡；丹麦；瑞典)
        
    - Hong Kong (China); Ireland; United Kingdom (中国香港；爱尔兰；英国)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - More comfortable with change and difference (面对变化与差异较为自在)
        

### Power Distance | 权力距离

_How acceptable is unequal power?｜多大程度接受权力不平等？_

#### High power distance | 高权力距离

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Unequal power and hierarchy are accepted (较认可权力不平等与等级结构)
        
    - Superiors give orders; subordinates obey (上级发号施令、下级服从)
        
    - Position and authority shape communication (身份地位与权威影响交往)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Philippines; Mexico; Venezuela (菲律宾；墨西哥；委内瑞拉)
        
    - India; Singapore; Brazil (印度；新加坡；巴西)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - A subordinate usually does not challenge a boss (下属通常不公开质疑上级)
        

#### Low power distance | 低权力距离

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Prefer relatively equal relationships (倾向相对平等的人际关系)
        
    - Ask for reasons; authority needs legitimacy (可以追问理由；权威须具正当性)
        
    - Expertise matters more than mere position (重视专业能力而不只是职位)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Austria; Israel; Denmark (奥地利；以色列；丹麦)
        
    - New Zealand; Sweden; Norway (新西兰；瑞典；挪威)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - Employees may question managerial decisions (员工可以质疑管理决定)
        

### Masculinity vs. Femininity | 男性气质与女性气质

_Achievement or care?｜强调成就还是关怀？_

#### Masculinity | 男性气质

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Achievement, competition and material success (重视成就、竞争与物质成功)
        
    - Gender roles more clearly separated (社会性别角色界限较明显)
        
    - Work-centered; recognition and advancement (以工作为中心；追求认可与晋升)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Japan; Austria; Italy (日本；奥地利；意大利)
        
    - Venezuela; Switzerland; Mexico (委内瑞拉；瑞士；墨西哥)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - “Live in order to work” (“为工作而生活”)
        

#### Femininity | 女性气质

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Caring, relationships and quality of life (强调关怀、人际关系与生活质量)
        
    - Gender roles overlap; value modesty (性别角色重叠；重视谦逊)
        
    - Cooperation and work–life balance (强调合作与工作生活平衡)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化**
    
    - Sweden; Norway; Netherlands (瑞典；挪威；荷兰)
        
    - Denmark; Finland (丹麦；芬兰)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - “Work in order to live” (“为生活而工作”)
        

## Edward T. Hall | 1 dimension / 2 types

### Context–Culture Theory | 语境文化理论

_Where does communicative meaning reside?｜意义主要由什么承载？_

#### High-context culture | 高语境文化

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Meaning relies on shared background and context (意义依赖共同背景和具体语境)
        
    - Indirect expression, silence and nonverbal cues (表达含蓄，重视沉默和非语言信号)
        
    - Relationship, status and implicit understanding (重视关系、地位与心照不宣)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化** _[外部教材补充]_
    
    - China; Japan (中国；日本)
        
    - South Korea; other examples vary by source (韩国；其他国家可能因资料而异)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - “Read between the lines” (理解言外之意)
        

#### Low-context culture | 低语境文化

- **Cultural features | 文化特征**
    
    - Explicit verbal messages carry meaning (意义主要通过明确语言传达)
        
    - Direct, precise statements and detailed information (表达直接、明确、信息更详尽)
        
    - Less dependence on assumed shared background (不依赖预先共享的文化背景)
        
- **Representative cultures | 代表国家／文化** _[外部教材补充]_
    
    - United States; Germany (美国；德国)
        
    - Switzerland; Scandinavian cultures (瑞士；斯堪的纳维亚文化)
        
- **Examples / expressions | 典型事例／表达**
    
    - “Say what you mean” (直接说出想表达的内容)
